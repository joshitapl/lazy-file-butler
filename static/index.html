What to expect

Preview first. AI suggestions are selected by default. File-type and manual placements need your explicit selection.

Undo with checks. Original paths and file hashes are recorded. If an organized file has changed, undo stops to protect your edits.

Bounded processing. Up to 100 files per batch. Content analysis is limited to files under 80 MB and sampled excerpts. Large files can still be sorted by type.

Offline after setup. The installed model and OCR run on your laptop. Local previews and extracted text are cached to avoid repeated work.
